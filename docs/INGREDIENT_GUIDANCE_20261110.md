# General ingredient guidance expansion

The deployed report previously selected only glycerin and panthenol for the dryness card, had general-function evidence for three ingredient families, and always rendered an empty caution card. The report now uses the versioned `data/reviewed_guidance.json` rules and 36 explicitly named Korean/English claim rows covering 16 ingredient families.

General-function cards use the bundled reviewed statements. This prevents a model omission from hiding an available function and prevents model prose from changing a function into a personal efficacy claim. The original six YSW rows and their source provenance are retained. Expanded rows have their own provenance; independent human review is still incomplete.

The caution card shows conditional label-use guidance for fragrance, eight individually named fragrance allergens, ethanol, retinol/retinal, glycolic/lactic acid, and salicylic acid. Guidance does not determine the uploaded product's safety or an individual's allergy. Ethanol and retinoid guidance is conditioned on selected dryness/sensitivity concerns or dry/sensitive skin type. Exact aliases are used: fatty alcohols, phenoxyethanol, glycols, similarly named extracts and other hyaluronate derivatives are not inferred from a substring. Citric acid is not automatically treated as an exfoliating product.

Known general-function ingredients are no longer incorrectly repeated in the additional-evidence card. Partial, unreadable and mock extractions cannot populate classifications. Model and user inputs cannot add trusted evidence or authorize mixing.

Sources checked on 2026-11-10 include the primary CIR reports linked in provenance, Cosmetics Europe's COSMILE ingredient records, [AAD dry-skin guidance](https://www.aad.org/public/everyday-care/skin-care-basics/dry/dermatologists-tips-relieve-dry-skin/), [AAD contact-dermatitis causes](https://www.aad.org/public/diseases/eczema/types/contact-dermatitis/causes), [European Commission fragrance-labelling guidance](https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/sectors/cosmetics/cosmetic-products-specific-topics/fragrance-allergens-labelling_en), and [FDA AHA](https://www.fda.gov/cosmetics/cosmetic-ingredients/alpha-hydroxy-acids) / [BHA guidance](https://www.fda.gov/cosmetics/cosmetic-ingredients/beta-hydroxy-acids). The niacinamide tone concern link cites [the 2002 clinical study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12100180/) and explicitly does not transfer its concentrations or formulation to the uploaded product.

No ingredient images, user keys or live test records belong in the public repository. The new Korean-brand corpus is local, separate from the earlier mixed personal-care corpus. A source-page screenshot is not represented as a photographed package. Production publication and live Gemini tests must be reported separately from offline checks.
